# CUSTOMER-ANALYSIS-FOR-RETAIL
This project focuses on analyzing customer transaction data for a retail business to generate insights into customer behavior, product performance, and sales trends. The goal is to use data analytics techniques to help retailers better understand their customers and improve business decision-making.
📁 Dataset Information

The dataset contains three sheets from the Excel file:

1️⃣ Customer

Contains demographic information about customers.

Example fields:

customer_id

DOB

Gender

City_code

2️⃣ Transactions

Contains customer purchase and return transactions.

Example fields:

transaction_id

cust_id

tran_date

prod_cat_code

prod_subcat_code

Qty

Rate

Tax

total_amt

Store_type

3️⃣ Product Hierarchy

Contains product category and subcategory details.

Example fields:

prod_cat_code

prod_cat

prod_sub_cat_code

prod_subcat

🎯 Business Problem

A retail company wants to analyze day-to-day customer transactions across different cities and product categories. The objective is to generate insights related to customer purchasing patterns, product popularity, store performance, and revenue generation.

🔍 Project Tasks
1️⃣ Data Preparation

Merge Customer, Transactions, and Product Hierarchy datasets.

Create a unified dataset called Customer_Final.

2️⃣ Data Exploration

Identify column names and data types.

Display Top 10 and Bottom 10 observations.

Generate five-number summary for continuous variables:

Minimum

Q1

Median

Q3

Maximum

Generate frequency tables for categorical variables.

3️⃣ Data Visualization

Create histograms for continuous variables.

Create bar charts for categorical variables.

4️⃣ Business Analysis

Answer the following business questions:

Determine the time period of available transaction data.

Count transactions where total amount is negative.

Identify product categories popular among male and female customers.

Find which city has the maximum number of customers.

Determine which store type sells the maximum products (by value and quantity).

Calculate total revenue from Electronics and Clothing categories from Flagship stores.

Find revenue generated by Male customers under Electronics category.

Identify customers with more than 10 unique transactions after removing negative transactions.

For customers aged 25–35 years:

Total spending on Electronics and Books.

Total spending between 1 Jan 2014 – 1 Mar 2014.
